Our Journey

A Quarter-Century of Educational Growth

Tracing our roots and milestone achievements over the years.

Founded on January 7, 2001, in Turkauliya, East Champaran, Roshani Public School began its journey under the aegis of the Roshani Educational and Welfare Trust (R.E.W.T.).

What started as a primary education facility with a modest number of students has grown steadily into a fully fledged Senior Secondary School. Our commitment to quality education led to our affiliation with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE Affiliation No. 330943).

Today, we proudly offer comprehensive academic streams including Science, Commerce, and Humanities, catering to a vibrant student community. Over the years, Roshani Public School has graduated thousands of alumni who excel in higher education and professional fields across India.

Mr. Arun Kumar
Principal, Roshani Public School

Message from Principal Mr. Arun Kumar

Dear Parents, Students, and Well-Wishers,

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Roshani Public School. Since our inception on January 7, 2001, under the aegis of the Roshani Educational and Welfare Trust (R.E.W.T.), our mission has been to provide quality, value-based education accessible to every child in Turkauliya and the surrounding regions of East Champaran.

As a premier CBSE-affiliated senior secondary institution (Affiliation No. 330943), we bridge traditional values with modern educational innovation. Our classrooms are powered by DigiClass smart interactive boards, our science and computer laboratories provide hands-on practical research, and our sports & co-curricular programs ensure well-rounded development.

We believe that education is a collaborative journey involving students, teachers, and parents. We encourage our learners to ask questions, explore new horizons, and cultivate empathy, discipline, and leadership.

Roshani Educational and Welfare Trust (R.E.W.T.)

Roshani Public School operates under the registered governance of the Roshani Educational and Welfare Trust (R.E.W.T.). The trust is committed to transparent administration, continuous infrastructural enhancement, and educational development in East Champaran.

Board of Trustees: Provides strategic vision, resource management, and institutional governance.
Principal (Mr. Arun Kumar): Serves as the administrative head overseeing daily academic operations and student welfare.
Managing Committee: Includes experienced educationists, parent representatives, and CBSE nominees shaping policies.
Our Pedagogy

Educational Philosophy

A comprehensive pedagogical approach aligned with NEP 2020 and CBSE guidelines.

Basic Education System (BES)

A strong foundational framework designed to build essential cognitive and conceptual skills early on.

Foundational Literacy & Numeracy

Ensuring every child achieves proficient reading, writing, and arithmetic skills as a cornerstone for learning.

Experiential Learning

Moving beyond rote learning through hands-on lab experiments, interactive projects, and field demonstrations.

Continuous Evaluation (CCE)

A structured evaluation model assessing both academic achievement and co-curricular performance.

Remedial & Extension Support

Individualized remedial sessions for students needing assistance alongside enrichment for high achievers.

Faculty Development

Regular training workshops and upskilling programs to equip teachers with modern teaching tools.
