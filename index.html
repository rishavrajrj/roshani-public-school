About Our School

A Legacy of Educational Excellence

Roshani Public School, located in Turkauliya, East Champaran, Bihar, is a premier co-educational senior secondary institution established in 2001 and affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE, Affiliation No. 330943).

Managed by the Roshani Educational and Welfare Trust (R.E.W.T.) under the guidance of Principal Arun Kumar, the school offers comprehensive education from Nursery through Class 12th. Our philosophy centers on providing a child-centric, holistic educational environment where academic excellence, moral values, and modern innovation coalesce.

Why Choose Roshani Public School

We are committed to nurturing future leaders who are academically competent, morally upright, socially responsible, and equipped to meet global challenges.

Academic Excellence

Strict adherence to CBSE curriculum with continuous and comprehensive evaluation, delivering outstanding board examination results.

Smart DigiClass Learning

Digitally enabled classrooms with interactive smart boards, multimedia learning software, and 3D visual learning modules for immersive education.

Holistic Development

Beyond academics — extensive sports, cultural activities, STEM robotics, fine arts, and co-curricular clubs to develop balanced personalities.

Value-Based Education

Instilling deep moral integrity, civic responsibility, respect for diversity, and emotional maturity alongside academic rigour.

Safe & Secure Campus

24/7 CCTV surveillance, trained security personnel, fire safety systems, and a comprehensive campus safety protocol.
