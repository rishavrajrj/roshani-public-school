HOW TO APPLY

Simple Steps to Admission

Our admission process is designed to be transparent and straightforward.

01

Registration

Obtain and submit the registration form either online or at the school counter.

02

Interaction/Assessment

Pre-Primary (Nursery-UKG): Informal interaction.
Class I to IX & XI: Written assessment in English, Hindi, Mathematics, and Science.

03

Document Verification & Confirmation

Submit required documents and pay fees to confirm admission.

Fee Policy

  • Admission Fee is payable once at the time of admission.
  • Tuition & Composite Fee can be paid monthly or quarterly by the 10th.
  • Lab & Computer Fee applies for practical subjects.
  • Payment Modes: Online (Net Banking/UPI/Cards) or at the school accounts desk.

Parent Guidelines

  • Inspect school diary daily.
  • Ensure punctuality and attendance.
  • Attend all Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs).
  • Download the Parent App from Play Store.
  • Ensure fee payment by the 10th of each month.
  • Visitors must register at security gate with valid photo ID.
